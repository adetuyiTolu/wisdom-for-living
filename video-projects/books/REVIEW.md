# Delivery review

The selected film turns an idea into a physical object: assembled architectural
volumes for The Builders and two illustrated book jackets for Books. Documentary
production footage and a continuous seed journey were the other directions;
neither was built because the supplied material contains no production footage
and the object story communicates the offering more directly.

Both full HyperFrames checks pass with zero runtime, layout, contrast, motion,
craft or audio findings. Phone sheets and scene-cut strips were inspected.
A separate visual reviewer found no remaining P0 after revisions. Corrections
include fading outgoing text before end cards, larger phone copy, a closer-to-wide
model progression, and clean separation between artwork and supporting text.

The end card holds deliberately for reading. Book titles have explicit reading
holds. Off-canvas sheet, spine and stage geometry is marked narrowly where it is
intentional. No check warnings remain.

The final files are H.264/AAC at 1920×1080, 30fps, 900 video frames, exactly 30
seconds, with MP4 metadata placed before the media for web playback. DELIVERY.json
records measured loudness and true peak. Sound is measured, not heard by the agent.
Decoded frames at 0, 6, 14, 20 and 27 seconds are checked before delivery.

Weakest shot: The Builders still uses an architectural metaphor. Authentic footage
of teams solving an economic problem would make the reality-series promise more
convincing. Books uses illustrative jackets, not approved production covers.
